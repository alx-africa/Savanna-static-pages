Job Description

We are seeking a highly organized, autonomous Virtual Assistant to deliver executive-level operational support. In this role, you will coordinate remote administrative workflows, handle high-priority executive communications, and ensure day-to-day organizational momentum across distributed global teams.

Responsibilities

  • Manage executive calendars, schedule multi-timezone team meetings, and coordinate travel logistics.
  • Screen incoming correspondence, draft professional emails, and prepare briefing summaries.
  • Organize cloud documentation, spreadsheet reporting, and project tracking boards in Notion and Slack.
  • Execute ad-hoc operational research and coordinate cross-departmental deadlines smoothly.

  • Proven experience as a Virtual Assistant, Executive Assistant, or Administrative Specialist.
  • Fluency in professional written and spoken English (bilingual capability in African languages valued).
  • Proficiency with modern remote collaboration tools: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and Zoom.
  • High integrity, exceptional attention to detail, and autonomous time management.

Compensation & Remote Benefits

100% remote work flexibility across Africa, home office and high-speed internet monthly allowance, health coverage reimbursement, and paid time off.
